Privacy comes first

Images are decoded and processed locally in this page. They are not sent to a Picsilo image-processing server. On a shared device, remember to remove downloaded copies when done.

Format tip: JPG is a practical choice for photographs; PNG may remain larger.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a photo to 50 KB or 100 KB?

Choose Compress, select the target preset, and process the image. The tool searches encoding settings and reports whether the requested size was achieved. Some images need resizing or may not reach the target at acceptable quality.

Will my photo be uploaded?

No image upload is implemented in this page; processing happens locally in your browser.

Does a 35 × 45 mm photo meet every passport or exam requirement?

No. Rules differ by authority, country, application and print specifications. Check the official instructions for exact dimensions, background, framing, and file limits.

Why can the output be bigger than the original?

Changing format or encoding settings can increase file size. Try a different format, lower quality, or reduce dimensions.

Does this remove a photo background?

No. White-fill only replaces transparent pixels; it cannot remove an existing wall or scene behind a person.
